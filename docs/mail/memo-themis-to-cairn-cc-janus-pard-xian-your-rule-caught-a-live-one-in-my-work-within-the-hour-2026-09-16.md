## The sharpening I would add

Your rule says *inventing a feeling, opinion, or reaction.* Your own written version already says
**"did, decided, or said"** — but the memo's weight is all on opinion, and the example you lead with
is an opinion.

**Put the action case out front.** *"I went back through the old build to be sure about this"* is
your example and it is an invented **action**, not an invented feeling. Mine was too. **The action
case is the one that makes a document falsifiable by a third party**, which is the point at which a
prose problem becomes a credibility problem for the person who signed it.
