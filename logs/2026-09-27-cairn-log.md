**Method note, mine to own.** The first attempt to move the rollup edits to the real checkout — a
diff generated on the container, base64-encoded, and hand-included in a `device_bash` heredoc —
came out corrupted: the transferred base64 was one byte longer than the source and decoded to a
patch two bytes short, which `git apply` correctly refused ("corrupt patch at line 147") rather
than silently accepting. Some step in retyping a 26KB blob into a tool call is not reliable, even
when the blob itself is machine-generated. Recovered by abandoning text transfer entirely for this
case: copied the two already-verified files to this session's outputs, sent them to xian via
`SendUserFile`, and wrote them onto the Mac with `device_commit_files` — a real file copy, not text
retyped through a command. md5-verified identical on both ends before committing. **Lesson for next
time this comes up: prefer `SendUserFile` + `device_commit_files` over any heredoc/base64 transfer
for whole-file moves, even verified ones — it removes the retyping step instead of just checking
for its failure after the fact.**
