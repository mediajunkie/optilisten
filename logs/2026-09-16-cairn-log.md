## ~13:0x — xian on 2.0 (3), and the call to stop

*"It did not crash immediately the way the other did but it did fail to start tracking anything and
it did crash eventually in a similar way to before."* His read: whack-a-mole; look for antipatterns
in the architectural model or the execution.

**Stopped fixing and read the whole capture path** — `LiveMicSource`, `PracticeLoopView`,
`CalibrationView`, `HomeView` — instead of producing a fourth theory. Writeup:
`docs/architecture-review-2026-09-16.md`.

**The finding, in one line:** `PracticeLoopView.swift:198` is `try? await source.start()`, and
`start()` has four throw sites. All four are discarded there. The view renders `currentShare`,
which is `0` when no buffer ever arrives — **a failed start is pixel-identical to a working start
in a quiet room.** "Tracked nothing" is the correct observable behaviour of this code when
`start()` throws, and which of the four threw is unknowable from outside the process.

`CalibrationView` is the same one level worse: `(try? …) ?? -20` / `?? -50` construct a
`Calibration` numerically identical to `.unavailable` — and **`.unavailable.isUsable` is `true`**,
30 dB gap against an 8 dB threshold. Total calibration failure is indistinguishable from success.

**Root cause: nothing in this loop has ever required the app to report its own state.** Written
without a device, verified by reading, compiled on a machine that can't run it, shipped to a tester
whose only instrument is "it crashed." With no observability every defect must be diagnosed by
inference from source — **which is exactly where Pard and I have each failed three times this
month.** The architecture and the process share one root, and that is why it feels like
whack-a-mole.

Four more defects found on the way, none proposed as the crash: `stop()` unreachable after a failed
`start()`; `observeInterruptions()` adding an unremovable observer per `start()`; time accounting as
buffer-count × 0.1s rather than elapsed seconds; one `AVAudioEngine` with two owners and no
arbitration.

**Next build reports rather than fixes** — `CaptureState` enum with `.failed(String)`, every `try?`
and `??` in the capture path rendered, a rolling in-app event log xian can copy out. Memo to Pard
and Janus asking for the new crash submission **and its text**, and asking him explicitly not to
build yet.

Also put the release valve on the table for the first time: **live capture is required by neither
compliance nor the product argument** — the loop closes on intention + reflection, `ManualSource`
exists, `Practice.isComplete` ignores measurement. A build with capture behind a flag satisfies
Apple and takes the microphone off the critical path. Not a recommendation; an option that should
exist in September rather than be discovered in November.
